Research

A researcher designing next-generation thermoelectric materials, with a strong foundation in chemistry. The focus: heat and charge transport in low-dimensional halide perovskites, steered through chemical bonding, lattice dynamics and phonon engineering.

  • Thermoelectric materials
  • Halide perovskites
  • Metal chalcogenides
  • Chalcohalides
  • Phonon engineering
  • Lattice dynamics
  • Wearable energy harvesting
02

Heat, slowed down

A thermoelectric device makes voltage from a temperature difference. If heat rushes straight through the material, that difference disappears. Switch the material and watch the voltage.

HOT SIDE COLD SIDE Tap or swipe sideways to add heat
Heat leak (κ)1.00
Voltage out (∝ ΔT)0%

Ordinary crystal. Lattice vibrations (phonons) carry heat straight across, so the hot and cold sides even out and very little voltage is produced.

Illustrative model, not a simulation of real materials
